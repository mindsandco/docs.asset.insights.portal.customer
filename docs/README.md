# Asset Insights Portal

**One trusted source for every asset you own and operate.**

Most organizations already hold the data they need to run their assets well. The problem is that the data lives in different systems, protocols, and vendors, so no one has the full picture in one place. Asset Insights Portal connects to your assets at the edge, harmonizes their data into one consistent model, and distributes it through open APIs, so every team and system works from a single source of truth they can rely on.

Asset Insights Portal is built by Antire, a people-first AI consultancy. Born in AI. Built for people. We partner with our customers to bridge their goals with trusted AI, and this portal is one of the ways we do it.

> Bridging your goals with trusted AI.

## Why teams choose it

- **Connect any asset.** Bring data in from many vendors, protocols, and sites over the standards your equipment already speaks.
- **Harmonize it into one model.** Automatic harmonization, rule-based validation, and signed delivery mean the data people act on is clean, consistent, and genuine.
- **Distribute it to every system.** Harmonized data is available through open APIs, so your analytics, ERP, and operational systems can use it straight away.
- **Act on what it tells you.** Templated notifications reach the right people through the right channel when something in the field needs attention.
- **Grow without friction.** The platform scales with your asset base, from a single site to a global estate, on the cloud or on-premise.
